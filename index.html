01 About the group

One group. Independent brands.

M&G Group is the umbrella identity for a portfolio of businesses led by Kevyn Shorey. Each company serves its own market, keeps its own customer relationships and trades under its own brand.

The group supplies the common ground: leadership, operating standards and shared systems. That leaves every business free to focus on the people it serves.
